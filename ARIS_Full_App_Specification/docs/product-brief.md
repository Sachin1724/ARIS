# Product Brief

ARIS is a friendly, mascot-led environmental companion for an Arduino UNO Q air-monitoring device. It explains local air quality, trends and forecasts in human language, with weather/traffic as optional context.

## First release
- Home dashboard and expressive lavender mascot.
- Current readings with units, timestamps, validity and calibration state.
- Trends, forecast readiness and available forecasts.
- Optional weather/traffic context with provider and freshness.
- Text Ask ARIS; voice can follow after text works.
- BLE discovery and secure Wi-Fi provisioning.
- Local REST/WebSocket connection, settings and diagnostics.

## Principles
Warm, not alarmist; explain recommendations; disclose uncertainty; no fabricated readings; local-first operation. No cloud accounts, remote access, medical diagnosis, guaranteed source attribution, Google Home/Matter, or actuator control in the initial scope.
